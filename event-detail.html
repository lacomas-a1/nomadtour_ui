Marafa (Hell's Kitchen) Sunset Tour

Discover the spectacular sandstone canyon, rich local legends and unforgettable sunset colours at Marafa Depression.

Tour Overview

Scheduled Departure: Every Friday

Pickup Locations: Kilifi, Watamu, Malindi

Duration: Half-day (approx. 4–5 hours) – afternoon to sunset

Group size: Max 10 travelers for an intimate experience

Journey into the heart of Marafa – also known as Hell's Kitchen – a stunning sandstone canyon carved by centuries of erosion. Walk through the winding gorges, hear the legends of the local Mijikenda people, and witness the dramatic colour shift as the sun sets over the red, orange and white rock formations. This tour is perfect for photographers, nature lovers, and culture enthusiasts.

Photography paradise Eco-friendly experience Sunset timing

The canyon glows at golden hour – a photographer's dream

Your Journey

1. Pickup & Scenic Drive

Afternoon pickup from your hotel in Kilifi, Watamu or Malindi (exact time shared upon booking). Enjoy a scenic drive through rural villages, coconut plantations, and baobab-dotted landscapes as we head inland toward Marafa.

2. Canyon Walk & Legends

Arrive at Marafa and begin a guided walk through the winding gorges. Learn about the Mijikenda legends behind the canyon's name "Hell's Kitchen" – including the story of a young couple who fled into the gorges. Marvel at the sculpted sandstone pillars and the deep, narrow fissures.

3. Sunset Viewpoint

As the sun begins to dip, take your position at the prime viewpoint overlooking the canyon. Watch the colours shift from warm ochre to deep crimson and violet as the light plays across the sandstone formations. This is the moment that makes Marafa unforgettable.

4. Return & Drop-off

After sunset, we head back as dusk settles over the coastal landscape. Arrive back at your hotel in the evening, with memories of the blazing sky and the ancient stories of Marafa.
