Vipingo Ridge Escape

5 days of luxury coastal living, white sands, marine life, and world-class golf – the ultimate Indian Ocean retreat.

Event Overview

Dates: December 10 – 15, 2026 (5 days / 4 nights)

Group size: Max 8 travelers for intimate experience

Experience the pinnacle of coastal luxury at Vipingo Ridge, Kenya's premier golf and beach resort. Located on the beautiful north coast, this exclusive destination offers pristine white sands, crystal-clear waters, and Africa's only PGA Baobab Course. This event combines world-class golf with marine adventures: snorkel over coral reefs, spot dolphins, explore coastal forests, and immerse yourself in Swahili culture. Ideal for golf enthusiasts, beach lovers, and those seeking a truly luxurious tropical getaway.

Best Time to Visit
December – March offers the best beach weather: hot, dry, and sunny with calm seas. July – October is also great with cooler temperatures and excellent visibility for snorkeling. The long rains (April–May) are best avoided.

Your Journey Day by Day

Day 1: Arrival – Vipingo Ridge

Morning pickup from Mombasa International Airport or Malindi airstrip. Transfer to the exclusive Vipingo Ridge resort with a welcome drink. Settle into your luxury ocean-view villa, then enjoy a leisurely afternoon on the white sands. Welcome dinner with Swahili cuisine under the stars.

Day 2: Marine Safari & Snorkeling

Boat trip to nearby marine parks. Snorkel among colorful coral reefs, spot sea turtles, and if you're lucky, dolphins. Picnic lunch on a sandbar. Afternoon at leisure, optional dhow sunset cruise.

Day 3: PGA Baobab Course & Golf

Experience Africa's only PGA-rated golf course – the stunning Baobab Course. Enjoy a round of golf with breathtaking ocean views, baobab trees, and challenging fairways. Afternoon at leisure: spa treatments, beach relaxation, or a second round. Evening sundowner at the clubhouse.

Day 4: Culture & Beach Activities

Visit a local Swahili village, learn about traditional fishing and coconut weaving. Afternoon choose your adventure: kite surfing, paddleboarding, or simply relax with a massage. Farewell beach barbecue with fresh seafood.

Day 5: Departure

Last morning swim and breakfast with ocean views. Transfer to Mombasa International Airport or Malindi airstrip for your onward journey. Carry the spirit of the coast with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a group tour or a private experience?
We offer both options to suit your travel style. Our scheduled departures are limited to small groups of up to eight guests, providing a more personalized experience than large coach tours. If you prefer complete flexibility, privacy, or are travelling with family, friends, or colleagues, we can arrange a fully private tour with your own guide, vehicle, and customized itinerary.
Can I extend my stay after the tour?
Absolutely. Many of our guests choose to extend their Kenyan holiday after the main itinerary. We can organize additional nights in Kilifi, Watamu, Diani, Malindi, Lamu, or Nairobi, together with airport transfers, accommodation, extra excursions, domestic flights, and customized activities to make the most of your visit.
Is golf equipment included?
Golf club hire is not included but can be arranged at the resort. We recommend bringing your own clubs for the best experience. Golf buggies are included in the green fee for one round.
What should I pack for this experience?
We recommend packing lightweight clothing, comfortable walking shoes, swimwear, sunglasses, sunscreen, a wide-brim hat, insect repellent, and a reusable water bottle. Golf attire is required on the course. A camera or binoculars will help you capture unforgettable wildlife and coastal moments.
Is snorkeling equipment provided?
Yes. For experiences that include snorkeling, we provide quality snorkeling equipment, including masks, snorkels, and fins. Safety briefings are conducted before every excursion, and experienced guides accompany guests throughout the activity to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.
Are meals included in the tour?
Meal inclusions vary depending on your chosen itinerary. Many of our full-day tours include lunch, while multi-day packages often include breakfast and selected meals. Your quotation will clearly indicate everything included in your package before you confirm your booking.
How do I book this experience?
Booking is simple. Complete the inquiry form on this page or contact our travel consultants directly. Once we understand your travel dates, preferences, and group size, we'll prepare a personalized itinerary and quotation. Your booking is confirmed once the required deposit has been received.
What happens if my travel plans change?
We understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. Our team will work with you to reschedule your itinerary or make adjustments wherever possible, subject to supplier terms and availability. We recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance for additional peace of mind.
