Magical Coastal Escape

Madaraka Day Weekend • May 29 – June 1, 2026
Three tiers of coastal luxury: ELITE, VOYAGER, NOMAD – curated experiences for every traveller.

Event Overview

Dates: Friday, May 29 – Monday, June 1, 2026 (4 days / 3 nights)

Celebrate Madaraka Day with an immersive coastal journey: from enchanting creeks and historic Malindi to the surreal Mambrui sand dunes and fiery Marafa canyon. Every moment is crafted for exclusivity: private dhow at sunset, floating restaurant dinner, farm-to-table feasts, and a grand Madaraka Day brunch.

The Curated 4‑Day Journey

Day 1: Arrival & The Enchanted Creek

VIP check‑in at your chosen accommodation (Elite Waterfront Villa / Kilifi Bay Standard / Boutique Glamping). Private lunch overlooking the bay. Sunset: private dhow cruise on Kilifi Creek with curated cocktails & appetizers. Dinner at a floating restaurant on the creek – grilled lobster & coastal delicacies.

Day 2: Heritage, History & The Golden Dunes

Guided tour of Malindi Museum, Vasco da Gama Pillar, and Old Town. Gourmet Italian‑Swahili fusion lunch at a boutique beachfront club. Afternoon expedition to Mambrui Sand Dunes – private butler‑serviced sundowner atop the dunes where desert meets the sea.

Day 3: Nature’s Majesty & The Crimson Canyon

Private safari in Arabuko Sokoke Forest (expert naturalist, endemic birds/ wildlife). Farm‑to‑table lunch near Watamu. Late afternoon: Marafa Hell’s Kitchen – witness “Golden Hour” as canyon walls glow deep red. Candlelit “Farewell Feast” on a private beach at the resort.

Day 4: Madaraka Day Celebration & Departure

Leisurely breakfast, spa treatment or paddleboarding in the bay. Midday: Grand Madaraka Day Brunch – coastal‑fusion fine dining. Afternoon private executive transfer to Malindi Airport or Mombasa SGR station.
