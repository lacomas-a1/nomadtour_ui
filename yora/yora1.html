Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the YORA summit package?
All packages include private safari, accommodation, meals, transfers, and park fees. Golf and marine activities are included in post‑summit packages.
Can I combine pre and post summit experiences?
Yes, the Full Explorer combines both pre‑summit safari and post‑summit beach & golf with all transfers.
How do I get from Nairobi to Kilifi?
We arrange private road transfers or domestic flights. Both options are included in our packages.
Are the safaris private?
Yes, all our safaris are private with your own vehicle and guide – no sharing with strangers.
