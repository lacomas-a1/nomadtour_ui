Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide airport transfers in Kenya?
Yes. We provide reliable private airport transfers from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) and Wilson Airport to hotels, conference venues, safari lodges, and beach resorts across Kenya. Our professional drivers monitor your flight, assist with luggage, and ensure you arrive safely and comfortably. Whether you are travelling alone, with family, or as part of a delegation, we have luxury sedans, executive SUVs, and spacious vans to suit your needs.
Can you help me find accommodation near GICC?
Absolutely. We partner with carefully selected 4-star and 5-star hotels located close to the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (GICC). Whether you are attending YORA or another event, we'll recommend accommodation based on your budget, preferred amenities, and travel schedule. Many of our partner hotels offer airport transfers, business facilities, restaurants, fitness centres, and executive lounges.
What safari experiences can I book?
We offer tailor-made safari experiences to Kenya's most iconic destinations including Masai Mara, Amboseli National Park, Tsavo East and West, Samburu National Reserve, Lake Nakuru, and Ol Pejeta Conservancy. Packages can include luxury accommodation, park entry fees, professional guides, private transport, game drives, domestic flights, and cultural experiences. Whether you have a weekend or two weeks, we'll design an itinerary that fits your schedule.
Can I combine my business trip with a beach holiday?
Yes. Many travellers extend their stay with a relaxing escape to Kenya's beautiful coastline. We organize personalized experiences in Kilifi, Watamu, Diani, Malindi, and Lamu, where you can enjoy white sandy beaches, snorkeling, dhow cruises, marine parks, Swahili culture, luxury resorts, and unforgettable sunsets before returning home.
Can you create a customized travel itinerary?
Yes. Every itinerary we create is personalized to your travel dates, interests, budget, and preferred pace. We can arrange airport transfers, accommodation, conference transportation, safari tours, domestic flights, coastal holidays, cultural experiences, and special requests, allowing you to enjoy a seamless journey from arrival to departure.
Do you organize travel for groups and corporate delegations?
Certainly. We manage travel for individuals, families, tour operators, conference delegates, NGOs, and corporate groups. Our services include accommodation booking, airport transfers, event logistics, private transportation, guided tours, and customized safari experiences, ensuring every member of your group enjoys a smooth and well-organized trip.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept secure payments in USD, EUR, and KES via bank transfers and major credit or debit cards. A deposit is typically required to confirm your booking, with the balance payable before your arrival. Our team will provide a detailed quotation and payment schedule for complete transparency.
Will I receive support during my stay in Kenya?
Yes. Our team provides dedicated support before, during, and after your trip. From answering travel questions and coordinating transportation to adjusting itineraries and assisting with unexpected situations, we're always available to ensure your experience in Kenya is smooth, enjoyable, and stress-free.
