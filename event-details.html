The Great Migration Encounter

5 days of witnessing nature's greatest spectacle – river crossings, vast herds, and the raw beauty of Maasai Mara.

Event Overview

Dates: July 15 – 20, 2026 (5 days / 4 nights)

Group size: Max 8 travelers for intimate experience

Capture the moment when over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebra, and gazelle thunder across the Mara River, facing crocodiles and predators. This event places you at prime crossing points with expert guides who have decades of migration-tracking experience. Beyond the crossings, enjoy sunrise game drives, bush dinners, and a visit to a Maasai village.

Photography friendly Eco-certified camp

Your Journey Day by Day

Day 1: Arrival – Nairobi to Mara

Morning pickup from your Nairobi hotel or airport. Fly by light aircraft to the Maasai Mara (approx. 45 min). Settle into our exclusive eco-camp overlooking the Talek River. After lunch, embark on an introductory game drive to spot big cats and elephants before a welcome dinner under the stars.

Day 2: Full Day – Mara River Crossings

Early breakfast and depart with packed lunch to strategic points along the Mara River. With guidance from local spotters, we position ourselves to witness dramatic river crossings. Return to camp late afternoon, optional sundowner drive. Evening talk on migration ecology.

Day 3: Hot Air Balloon & Private Concession

Pre-dawn departure for a once-in-a-lifetime hot air balloon safari (optional extra). Float above the savannah as herds move below. Champagne breakfast in the bush. Afternoon game drive in a private conservancy with fewer vehicles and night game drive possibilities.

Day 4: Cultural Immersion & Leopard Tracking

Morning visit to a Maasai village to learn traditional singing, jumping dances, and beadwork. After lunch, focus on elusive leopards along the Olare Orok river. Photography workshop with your guide. Farewell dinner with Maasai entertainment.

Day 5: Final Safari & Departure

Last morning game drive at sunrise, seeking any missed wildlife. Return to camp for brunch, then transfer to the airstrip for your flight back to Nairobi. Arrive late afternoon with memories of a lifetime.
