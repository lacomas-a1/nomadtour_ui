Who We Are

At Nomad Tours and Experiences, travel is more than visiting new places — it’s about creating stories, discovering cultures, and experiencing the beauty of Africa in a meaningful way. For over 2 years, we have built a trusted reputation for authentic, responsible, and unforgettable travel experiences.

From the wildlife of Maasai Mara and the peaks of Mount Kenya to serene coastlines and ocean adventures, we create personalized journeys that inspire wonder, connection, and lifelong memories. Every experience is carefully crafted and guided by deep local knowledge.

We believe travel is about connecting with nature, wildlife, people, and cultures. Through small group experiences, community partnerships, and expert local guides, we deliver meaningful adventures while supporting local communities and protecting the natural world for future generations.

Our Mission

To craft journeys that are authentic, responsible, and transformative, guided by our passionate team of travel experts, local guides, and storytellers.

Our Vision

To connect travelers with meaningful experiences that create lasting memories while positively impacting the communities we visit and the environments we explore.
