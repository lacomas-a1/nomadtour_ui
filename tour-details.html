Day-by-Day Safari Itinerary (7 Days / 6 Nights)

Day 1: Nairobi – Masai Mara
Pickup from NairobiGreat Rift Valley viewpointAfternoon game drive

Pick up from your Nairobi hotel at 7:00 AM. Drive through the Great Rift Valley, arriving at Masai Mara for lunch. Afternoon game drive in search of lions, elephants, and giraffes. Overnight at a luxury tented camp.

Mara Sopa Lodge

Perched on the slopes of Oloolaimutia Hills, this lodge offers stunning views of the Mara plains. Stone cottages with en-suite bathrooms, a swimming pool, and nightly cultural performances. Eco-friendly and perfectly positioned for early morning game drives.

Day 2: Full Day Masai Mara
Early morning game driveMaasai village visit (optional)Mara River

Early morning game drive (best for predators). Return for breakfast, then optional visit to a Maasai village. Afternoon game drive focusing on the Mara River – possible wildebeest crossings during migration season.

Mara Sopa Lodge (Second Night)

Continue your tour at the same lodge. Enjoy the pool, hearty buffets, and evening entertainment. The lodge's elevated position provides exceptional sunrise views over the savannah.

Day 3: Masai Mara – Lake Nakuru
Scenic drive to NakuruAfternoon game driveRhino sanctuary

Breakfast, then depart for Lake Nakuru National Park. Arrive for lunch, afternoon game drive around the lake – see flamingos, rhinos, and Rothschild giraffes. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Lodge.

Lake Nakuru Lodge

Set inside the park with panoramic lake views. Eco-lodge featuring a swimming pool, game deck, and excellent birdwatching terraces. Famous for its resident hippos and stunning sunset views over the alkaline lake.

Day 4: Lake Nakuru – Amboseli
Morning game driveDrive via NairobiEvening game drive in Amboseli

Morning game drive at Lake Nakuru, then drive to Amboseli National Park (via Nairobi for lunch). Arrive in Amboseli in the late afternoon, enjoy an evening game drive with views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Overnight at Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge

Thatched-roof rooms inspired by Maasai craftsmanship, overlooking a waterhole visited by elephants. Breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, swimming pool, and an award-winning restaurant serving Swahili cuisine.

Day 5: Full Day Amboseli
Morning & afternoon game drivesObservation HillMaasai community visit

Full day exploring Amboseli. Witness huge herds of elephants against the snow-capped peak of Kilimanjaro. Morning and afternoon game drives plus optional visit to a Maasai community.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge (second night)

Unwind after an exciting day of game drives. The lodge’s waterhole attracts wildlife in the evenings – perfect for photography. Enjoy a traditional Maasai dance by the bonfire.

Day 6: Amboseli (extra game drive)
Optional Tsavo West excursionBird watching

Another full day of game drives in Amboseli, or optional excursion to Tsavo West (Mzima Springs – extra cost). Enjoy the serene landscapes and prolific birdlife.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge (third night)

Final night at this iconic lodge. Relax by the pool, enjoy a sundowner at the observation deck, and capture the last sunset over Kilimanjaro before heading back to Nairobi.

Day 7: Amboseli – Nairobi
Final game driveDepart to NairobiAirport drop-off

After breakfast, a final game drive as you exit the park. Drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon. Drop-off at your hotel or airport for onward flight.
