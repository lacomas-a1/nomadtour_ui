7-Day Kenya Classic Safari

Daily departures

Nairobi – Masai Mara – Lake Nakuru – Amboseli – Nairobi

Max 6 people

Experience the best of Kenya on this 7-day safari across Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru, and Amboseli. Witness the Great Migration (seasonal), see pink flamingos at Nakuru, and photograph elephants with Mount Kilimanjaro in the background.

What's Included

Transport

4x4 Land Cruiser with pop-up roof

Accommodation

Luxury tented camps & lodges

Meals

Full board (breakfast, lunch, dinner)

Game Drives

Unlimited in all parks

Also included: Park fees, AMREF evacuation, bottled water on safari.

Day-by-Day Safari Itinerary (7 Days / 6 Nights)

Day 1: Nairobi – Masai Mara
Pickup from NairobiGreat Rift Valley viewpointAfternoon game drive

Pick up from your Nairobi hotel at 7:00 AM. Drive through the Great Rift Valley, arriving at Masai Mara for lunch. Afternoon game drive in search of lions, elephants, and giraffes. Overnight at a luxury tented camp.

Mara Sopa Lodge

Perched on the slopes of Oloolaimutia Hills, this lodge offers stunning views.

Day 2: Full Day Masai Mara
Early morning game driveMaasai village visit (optional)Mara River

Early morning game drive (best for predators). Return for breakfast, then optional village visit. Afternoon game drive focusing on the Mara River.

Mara Sopa Lodge (Second Night)

Continue your stay. Enjoy the pool and evening entertainment.

Day 3: Masai Mara – Lake Nakuru
Scenic drive to NakuruAfternoon game driveRhino sanctuary

Breakfast, then depart for Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive around the lake – see flamingos, rhinos, and Rothschild giraffes.

Lake Nakuru Lodge

Eco-lodge inside the park with panoramic lake views.

Day 4: Lake Nakuru – Amboseli
Morning game driveDrive via NairobiEvening game drive in Amboseli

Morning game drive at Lake Nakuru, then drive to Amboseli (via Nairobi). Evening game drive with views of Kilimanjaro.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge

Thatched-roof rooms overlooking a waterhole with Kilimanjaro views.

Day 5: Full Day Amboseli
Morning & afternoon game drivesObservation Hill

Full day exploring Amboseli. Witness huge herds of elephants against Kilimanjaro.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge (second night)

Relax after game drives, enjoy the waterhole views.

Day 6: Amboseli (extra game drive)
Optional Tsavo West excursionBird watching

Another full day of game drives or optional excursion to Tsavo West.

Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge (third night)

Final night at this iconic lodge. Sundowner views of Kilimanjaro.

Day 7: Amboseli – Nairobi (Return)
Final game driveDepart to NairobiAirport drop-off

After breakfast, a final game drive as you exit the park. Drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon. Drop-off at your hotel or airport – completing the full circuit.
